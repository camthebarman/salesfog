1. Your script

Script format
  • # Step title starts a new step. No headings? Each paragraph becomes a step.
  • # Objection: Not interested — your rebuttal for that button. Also Not the right time, Already using a competitor, or any custom objection.
  • Placeholders: {{prospect}} {{first_name}} {{company}} {{my_name}} {{my_company}} {{industry}} {{pos}} (POS system, detected from their website)
  • Any other placeholder, like {{local_bar}}, becomes a field you fill in before the call.
  • Industry words: {{customers}} {{customer}} {{venue}} {{team}} {{offering}} {{visit}} — e.g. a diner gets “regulars”, a cocktail bar gets “clients”. Capitalize to capitalize: {{Customers}}.
  • With auto-adapt on, plain words like “customers” are swapped too.
  • [diner, cafe] line shows a line only for those industries; [!diner] line hides it. Industries:
  • > note is a note to you, not read aloud. - item for bullets, **bold** for emphasis.
